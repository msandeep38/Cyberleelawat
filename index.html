About Us

Building a safer digital world through education and awareness

Cyber Leelawat is an ISO 9001:2015 Certified cyber education platform dedicated to empowering rural and Hindi-speaking learners. We provide industry-relevant, practical cybersecurity training that bridges the gap between classroom theory and real-world defense.

From Ethical Hacking to Bug Bounty Bootcamp, our courses are designed by experts to provide hands-on experience through live projects and internship opportunities.

Our mission goes beyond education; we actively conduct Cyber Awareness Programs across schools and colleges to build a safer, more resilient digital society for everyone.
